    Why now?

    XPS is Microsoft's abandoned "XML Paper Specification" document format. The built-in XPS Viewer was removed from Windows 10 and 11, and Macs, Chromebooks and Linux never opened it. Yet people still receive .xps files — invoices, statements, exports from the old "Microsoft XPS Document Writer" printer. XPSRescue turns them into PDF on any device.

    How it works

    1. Drop a .xps file (or a whole folder with the Batch Pass).
    2. Wait a second — the file is read and laid out as a PDF inside your browser.
    3. Download the PDF. Print it, share it, or archive it.

    Questions

    Are my files uploaded anywhere?

    No. Your XPS files never leave your device: the conversion runs inside your browser.

    What is an .xps or .oxps file?

    XPS (XML Paper Specification) is a fixed-layout document format from Microsoft, similar to PDF. OpenXPS (.oxps) is the standardised version. Both are supported.

    Can I try before paying?

    Yes — convert as many single files as you want for free. The Batch Pass only saves you from doing them one by one.

    A file failed — what now?

    Some unusual or damaged files may not convert cleanly yet. If you bought the Batch Pass and it does not work for your files, you get a full refund.
