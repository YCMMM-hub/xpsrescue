Privacy policy

Applies to the XPSRescue website and the XPSRescue browser extension.

Your files

Your XPS and OXPS files are converted inside your browser, on your own computer. They are never uploaded, stored or seen by us. The conversion makes no network request.

Data we collect

The extension collects no data at all: no analytics, no cookies, no account, no tracking. The only thing it stores on your device is, if you buy the Batch Pass and enter your code, a flag saying the code was accepted. It never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the extension.

The website may count page visits with an audience counter that does not use cookies and does not identify you.

Payment

Payment is optional and happens outside the extension: the “Buy” button opens a Stripe payment page in a new tab. Payments are processed by Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., under its own privacy policy. We receive the e-mail address and name you give at checkout, only to send your activation code and answer support requests. We never receive your card details.
